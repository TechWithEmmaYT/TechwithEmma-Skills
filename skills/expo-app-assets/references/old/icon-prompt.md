# Icon Prompt

Use this reference when a copy-ready image-generation prompt is needed.

The goal is a **sleek, premium consumer app icon**, not a corporate logo, cartoon illustration, or generic 3D object.

## Visual quality

Describe a sleek contemporary app-icon finish using clean sculpted forms, confident geometry, crisp silhouettes, controlled gradients, selective translucency, subtle depth, restrained lighting, clean edges, and generous visual breathing room.

Favor a few large, resolved forms over small details. Premium does not require glass or 3D; flat concepts should feel polished through proportion, spacing, silhouette, geometry, and color.

## Composition

Build around one dominant memorable idea. Let the subject crop, emerge, overlap, wrap, split, merge, float, transform, or create negative space. Avoid automatically centering a small object on a colored square. Make the background participate in the composition and keep the result readable at 32px.

## Creative freedom

Tell the image model the product, personality, desired quality, and creative territory, then let it invent the solution. For exploration boards, say: **INVENT THE CONCEPTS YOURSELF.**

Vary archetype, silhouette, composition, behavior, palette, and depth. The board should resemble several talented consumer-app teams answering the brief, not one coordinated icon pack.

## Avoid illustration drift

Avoid detailed faces, individual hair or eyelash detail, thick illustrative outlines, busy textures, poster scenes, stickers, clip art, generic clay 3D, excessive glass or chrome, excessive glow, stock category symbols, corporate marks, and repeated centered-symbol-on-gradient compositions.

Characters and recognizable objects are welcome, but simplify them into strong app-icon forms.

## Prompt shape

Keep prompts concise: app and purpose, personality, concept count, freedom to invent, diversity requirement, quality, composition and readability, presentation format, and a short exclusion list. Do not turn the prompt into a specification document.

## Exploration-board finish

Use a clean white presentation canvas with equal square cells, generous gutters, and small option numbers only. No captions, explanations, device mockups, or decorative presentation. Each concept should be high-resolution and crop-friendly.

## Final icon

After selection, prompt for the chosen concept alone. Preserve its identity and composition while increasing polish. Request a 1024×1024 square with one dominant subject, clean edges, simplified detail, no text, no option number, no device mockup, and no baked platform mask. Do not redesign the selected concept during final generation.
