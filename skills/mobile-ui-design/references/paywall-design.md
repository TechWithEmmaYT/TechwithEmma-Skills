# Paywall Design

Use this reference to design subscription, trial, upgrade, and plan-selection surfaces. Define the experience and handoff; do not choose or integrate a billing SDK.

## Place it after understandable value

Connect the paywall to what the user just achieved, previewed, or personalized. A standalone upgrade screen may instead begin with the premium capability the user tried to use. Do not interrupt a first-run journey before the product promise is understandable.

Choose one primary story:

- personalized result or plan;
- free-trial timeline;
- free-versus-premium comparison;
- plan or bundle choice;
- premium feature demonstration.

Do not cram all stories into one screen. Use a second state, sheet, or scroll section only when the information remains necessary.

## Build a clear hierarchy

Order the composition around:

1. close or skip when allowed, plus restore;
2. one outcome-led headline and short support;
3. a focused hero, proof point, comparison, or personalized summary;
4. the plan/trial choice with an unmistakable selected state;
5. one primary purchase action;
6. renewal, cancellation, terms, privacy, and price clarification.

Keep the CTA reachable without hiding the important conditions. A fixed footer may use a short surface fade over scrolling content. Use gradients for atmosphere or readability, not to make every plan card look premium.

## Make the offer trustworthy

Design the full intended experience: what is due today, trial duration, charge date, recurring amount and cadence, introductory versus regular pricing, cancellation conditions, restore, legal destinations, default selection, promotional urgency, and dismissal or exit states.

Before real commerce or content exists, use believable representative prices, savings, ratings, testimonials, trial dates, countdowns, eligibility, and offer states so the design is complete and presentation-ready. Do not place `mock`, `sample`, placeholder labels, or implementation warnings inside the UI. After presenting the design, tell the owner in chat that production must connect or replace these with the localized store catalog, real eligibility and expiration, computed savings, verified claims/reviews, billing actions, and entitlement state.

## Design real states

Cover the states the chosen experience needs: selected/unselected plan, trial on/off, alternate tier, expanded comparison or all-plans sheet, purchase pending, purchase error, restore result, ineligible offer, success, and dismissal. Treat alternate compositions as meaningful states, not cosmetic variants.

Keep motion restrained: immediate selection feedback, short crossfades or pill movement, native scrolling, and standard sheet behavior. Do not add a decorative mount animation by default. Provide a reduced-motion final state.

## Handoff

Record placement in the journey, value story, plans and default selection, trial rules, CTA labels, price disclosures, close/skip behavior, restore/legal actions, state transitions, representative content to replace, and unresolved commerce decisions in `mobile-design.md`.
